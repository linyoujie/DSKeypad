Privacy Policy for DS Keypad

Last updated: May 12, 2026

DS Keypad is a secondary-screen keyboard and touchpad utility designed mainly for AYANEO Pocket DS. This Privacy Policy explains how DS Keypad handles user data.

Data Collection

DS Keypad does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to our servers.

Input and Accessibility

DS Keypad may use Android Accessibility Service permission to send keyboard, touchpad, and gesture input at the user's request. This permission is used only to perform input actions on the user's device. The app does not record, upload, or share typed text, touch input, or screen content.

Local Storage

DS Keypad may store app settings locally on the device, such as keyboard layout preferences, touchpad settings, saved click positions, and display preferences. These settings remain on the user's device.

Network

DS Keypad does not require a server account and does not upload user data.

Children

DS Keypad is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact email listed on the DS Keypad Google Play Store page.
